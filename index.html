📋 Overview

Complete guide to the GUNPLA Workshop program

🤖 GUNPLA Workshop for Elementary School

An innovative STEM education program introducing students to plastic model kits (GUNPLA) and their manufacturing process.

Program Objectives

By assembling plastic models, students can experience the essence of "creating something" and appreciate the effort put into producing products.

Teacher's Guide

Comprehensive guide containing detailed class preparation instructions, learning objectives, and material lists.
